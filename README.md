## Do You Actually Need a Paid Registered Agent?

Not always. You can be your own registered agent if you have a street address in your formation state where someone can accept legal papers every business day. The Wyoming, Delaware and Florida forms we checked won't take a PO box on its own.

A paid service makes sense if you work from home and want that address off a public filing, can't guarantee someone is there during business hours, formed in a state you don't live in, or run companies in several states. Our guide on [being your own registered agent](https://persuasion-nation.com/can-i-be-my-own-registered-agent-for-my-llc/) covers the trade-offs.

**Keep in mind what an agent won't do.** It won't make you anonymous, since your principal office and member names may still be public. It isn't a business address. It won't forward all your mail. And none of these five files your annual report for you.

## FAQ

### What is a registered agent, and why does my LLC need one?

A registered agent is the person or company named in your state filings to receive service of process, tax notices and official state mail for your LLC. Every state requires LLCs and corporations to keep one, with a physical in-state address and availability during business hours.

### Is a registered agent the same as a statutory agent or resident agent?

Yes. Some states just use a different name. You'll see "statutory agent" in Ohio and Arizona and "resident agent" in Maryland and Nevada. Same job.

### What is the best registered agent service for an LLC?

For most single-state LLC owners, Northwest Registered Agent: $125 a year with no renewal increase, free switching, and portal cancellation. If you're forming a brand-new LLC, Bizee's free first year is hard to beat.

### How much does a registered agent cost per year?

Among these five, annual fees run from $99 to $199. Over three years, expect roughly $297 (Harbor prepaid) to $497 (ZenBusiness). Always compare the renewal price, not the intro price.

### What is the cheapest registered agent service?

Paying yearly, Northwest is cheapest of these five over three years at $375. Prepaying, Harbor ($297 for 3 years) and InCorp ($435 for 5 years) cost less, but neither refunds a prepaid term. Forming new? Bizee's included first year brings its 3-year total to $298.

### Can I be my own registered agent?

Yes, if you have a street address in your formation state and can reliably accept legal papers there during business hours. That address becomes part of the public record.

### Can I use a PO box or virtual mailbox as my registered office address?

No. States require a physical street address where papers can be handed over in person.

### Does a registered agent keep my home address private?

Only partly. It keeps your home address out of the registered agent field. Depending on your state, your principal office, mailing address and member or manager names may still show up on your articles of organization or annual report.

### Is a registered agent address the same as a business address?

No. It's for receiving legal and state documents. InCorp and ZenBusiness say their agent address isn't a business address, and InCorp charges $49 to use it that way.

### What happens if my LLC doesn't have a registered agent?

You can fall out of good standing and eventually face administrative dissolution. Worse, if you're sued and there's no valid agent to receive the papers, you might not hear about it until a default judgment arrives.

### Do I need a registered agent in every state?

You need one in your formation state and in every state where your LLC registers as a foreign LLC. Northwest drops to $100 per company at five or more, and InCorp charges $129 in every state.

### How do I change my registered agent?

File a change of registered agent form with your state (fees vary). Northwest pays that fee when you switch to it, and InCorp covers it up to what you paid InCorp. Check your old agent's cancellation terms first. Our guide on [how to change your registered agent](https://persuasion-nation.com/how-to-change-registered-agent/) walks through it.

### Does a registered agent file my annual report?

None of these five files it as part of the agent fee. Northwest, InCorp and Bizee send compliance reminders, but the filing and state fee are still on you.

### Which registered agent is best for non-US founders?

Any provider here can be your agent in your formation state. Just know an agent doesn't give you a US bank account, an EIN or a US business address. Our guide to [forming an LLC as a non-US resident](https://persuasion-nation.com/llc-for-non-us-residents/) covers the extra steps.

### Can I cancel my registered agent service anytime?

With Northwest, yes, from your client portal at no charge. Harbor wants 90 days' notice and proof of removal, InCorp wants proof before renewal, and ZenBusiness doesn't allow online cancellation.
